How to read this result:
  packets > 0 and framing MATCHES -> the transport contract holds,
        and Phase 3 can build on it directly.
  packets > 0 but framing MISMATCHES -> the driver delivers raw
        bytes, not HCIT packets. Worth knowing early: the port's
        framing layer may belong in userspace instead.
  0 packets -> expected if the chip needs the DM/PRM firmware
        sequence first. Next step is porting nfc_hal_dm.c.
